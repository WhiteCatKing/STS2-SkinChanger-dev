# Skin Changer 1.0.41.21 维修日志

覆盖 Steam 工坊公开版 1.0.41 之后到 Git 内测版 1.0.41.21 的改动。

## 中文

- 假商人「商人？？？」的事件背景可以在「其他图鉴」里单独切换。「跟随分类」优先使用确实替换了事件背景的模组，有多个来源时按皮肤优先级选择。事件背景与战斗背景各自应用。
- 假商人图鉴可预览购物界面，六件商品及价格与事件一致。修复购物时手部贴图串到其他皮肤、进场或切换时短暂显示原皮，以及战斗换肤后模型放大或出现白色矩形的问题。皮肤自带的战斗动画继续由作者模组播放。
- 局内可调整假商人战斗背景的位置和缩放。U 放大、O 缩小，I/J/K/L 分别向上、左、下、右移动。假商人事件与火堆中的玩家角色换肤会立即刷新，小红帽在假商人事件中使用皮肤作者提供的商店姿势。
- 主菜单悬停「继续游戏」时，存档小框使用与局内左上角一致的角色头像。
- 兼容「蒂菲拉·亡灵契约」。修复骨妹与奥斯提混搭时 Orchis 选人背景停在静帧，以及与另一款选人模组共存时预览小人残留原版特效的问题。选人专用控件在离开界面时清理，再进来时随已选皮肤恢复。
- 「其他图鉴」支持预览独立奥斯提皮肤「撑在地上的鸡煲」及其动作，并能识别「鲸鱼娘瓦片替换」的瓦皮外观。补齐瓦库部分皮肤在图鉴中缺失的插画。
- 改进第三方 PCK 和导入贴图识别。单个异常资源包扫描失败时会记录原因并继续检测其他皮肤。

## English

- Merchant??? now has a separate event background selector in the Other Compendium. Follow Category considers only mods that replace the event background and uses skin priority when several qualify. Event and combat backgrounds are applied separately.
- The Merchant??? shop preview shows the event’s six fixed items and prices. Fixed incorrect shop hand textures, the vanilla appearance flashing on entry or during a switch, and combat skins becoming oversized or turning into a white rectangle. Skins with their own combat animations retain those animations.
- Adjust the fake merchant combat background during a run with U/O to zoom in/out and I/J/K/L to move up/left/down/right. Player skins now refresh in the fake merchant event and at rest sites. Red Hood uses the shop pose supplied by the skin author.
- The Continue Run tooltip now uses the same character icon as the in-run top bar.
- Added Tifira Necrobinder compatibility. Fixed the Orchis selection background freezing with mixed Necrobinder and Osty skins and vanilla preview effects leaking in alongside another selection-screen mod. Selection-only controls are removed when leaving and restored with the selected skin when returning.
- The Other Compendium now previews the standalone Osty skin 撑在地上的鸡煲 and its actions, recognizes the Whale Girl Wuppy replacement, and fills in a missing Vakuu illustration.
- Improved third-party PCK and imported texture detection. If one pack fails to scan, the scanner logs the cause and continues with the remaining packs.
