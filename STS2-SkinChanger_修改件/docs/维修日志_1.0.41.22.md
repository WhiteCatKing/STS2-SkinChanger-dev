# Skin Changer 1.0.41.22 维修日志

## 中文

- 修复独立卡图包的先古牌选项在「先古」分类里丢失的问题。先合并卡图包，再将角色先古牌的选项和对应贴图挂到角色卡池与「先古」分类。其他模组已提供先古卡图时，原有卡图包仍可选择。
- 修复原版卡与新角色卡重名时漏掉原版美化的问题。卡图有明确卡池路径时按卡池匹配。只有文件名可用时，优先匹配唯一的原版卡。不同卡池里的同名卡分别从自己的卡池读取选项。
- 用本机「愚者卡面美化」包离线扫描，91 张图全部识别。先古分组和重名回归测试、完整运行时测试、逻辑测试和双版本检查均通过。两包同时启用时「先古之名」的游戏内显示仍待确认。

## English

- Fixed missing standalone art-pack options in the Ancient filter. Character Ancient cards now carry their art-pack options and exact textures into both the character pool and the Ancient filter, including when another mod supplies Ancient art.
- Fixed missed vanilla art when a custom character card shares its name. Paths with an explicit pool are matched to that pool. Unscoped filenames prefer a unique vanilla match. Same-name cards in different pools resolve their options separately.
- The installed Fool Card Art bundle passed offline recognition for all 91 images. Regression tests, the full runtime suite, logic tests, and stable/beta checks passed. The reported card still needs in-game confirmation with both packs enabled.
